Three things to know first

  • Centres are fixed. They define each face's colour. You don't choose where white goes — the cube does.
  • You solve in floors. Bottom layer, middle layer, top layer, in that order.
  • Three piece types: centres (1 sticker), edges (2 stickers, 12 total), corners (3 stickers, 8 total). A piece keeps its stickers — you're just moving them around.

Notation, one minute

Each letter turns that face 90° clockwise, looking straight at it.

R right
L left
U upper (top)
D down (bottom)
F front
B back
  • R' = right, counter-clockwise
  • R2 = right, twice (180°)

Warm up: the sexy move

The atom of beginner solving. Drill until it's muscle memory.

R U R' U'
1WHITE CROSS

Build the white cross

White plus on top, with side colours matching centres.

State after step 1. White is up. Each side shows its centre colour matching the edge above it.

Hold cube with white on top. Move each white edge into place so its other colour matches the centre it touches. No fixed algorithm — figure it out visually. Easiest path: bring the edge to the bottom layer, line it up under its target, then turn the side face twice to flip it up.

Wrong result: a "daisy" (white cross on top but side colours don't match centres). Keep going.
2WHITE CORNERS

Insert the white corners

Full white face. First-layer sides show solid colour bars.

State after step 2. Solid white on top. Each side has its top row as a matching colour bar.

Find a white corner in the bottom layer. Rotate D until it sits directly below its target slot. Hold at front-right-bottom, repeat sexy move:

REPEAT 1, 3, OR 5 TIMES R U R' U'
Corner stuck in top wrong? Sexy once to eject it down, then reinsert properly.
3MIDDLE LAYER

Solve the middle edges

First two layers complete. Only yellow (top) remains.

State after step 3. Cube is flipped (white now on bottom). Bottom two-thirds of every side solid. Only the top yellow layer is a mess.

Flip the cube so white is now on the bottom. Yellow is up. Find a top-layer edge with no yellow. Rotate U so its front colour matches the front centre (upside-down T). Then send it left or right:

EDGE GOES RIGHT U R U' R' U' F' U F
EDGE GOES LEFT U' L' U L U F U' F'
Edge trapped wrong in middle? Kick it out with either algorithm, then reinsert.
4YELLOW CROSS

Make the yellow cross

Yellow plus on top. Ignore side colours.

Look at the yellow face. You'll see one of these four states:

Dot Run algo 3×
L-shape Hold L in top-left. 2×
Line Horizontal. 1×
Cross Done — skip to 5
F R U R' U' F'

Same algorithm each time. Progression is always dot → L → line → cross.

State after step 4. Yellow cross visible on top. Top corners still any colour.
5YELLOW FACE

Orient all yellow corners

Entire top face is yellow. Sides will still be messy.

State after step 5. Top is fully yellow. Top-row side stickers are scrambled — expected.

This is Sune. Never rotate the whole cube between reps — only turn U to bring the next unsolved corner to the front-left-top slot.

R U R' U R U2 R'
  • 0 yellows on top: hold with a yellow sticker on the front-left face. Sune. Look again.
  • 1 yellow on top: hold that corner at front-left-top. Sune. Look again.
  • 2 yellows on top: hold one at front-left-top. Sune. Look again.
It gets worse before better. Trust it — the yellow face will complete in 1–3 reps.
6CORNER SWAP

Position the corners

Each corner sits between its correct two centres.

State after step 6. Corners home — top-row corners of each side match the centre. Only top edges (middle of top row) still wrong.

Find a corner where the two side stickers match the two centres it sits between (yellow can point any direction). Hold that solved corner at front-right-top.

U R U' L' U R' U' L
No corner solved? Run once from any angle. One will now be correct — re-orient and run again.
7EDGE CYCLE

Position the last edges

Solved cube.

Final state — solved. All six faces one colour each.

Find the one already-solved top edge (its side colour matches its centre). Hold it at the back.

R U' R U R U R U' R' U' R2
No solved edge? Run once from any angle. One will lock in — move it to the back, run again.
