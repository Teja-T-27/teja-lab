OverviewWhat it is

The ES 350 is the V6 version of Lexus's mid-size executive sedan — the same platform as the Toyota Camry / Avalon, but retuned for softer damping, thicker glass, and a longer wheelbase. It has always been the quiet Lexus: not the sport one (that's the IS), not the flagship (that's the LS), just the one people keep for a decade because nothing goes wrong.

PowertrainUnder the hood

A naturally aspirated 2GR-FKS V6 sits transversely up front, driving the front wheels through an eight-speed automatic. No turbo, no hybrid battery on this trim — just a port-and-direct-injected six that has been in Toyota's parts bin for a decade, which is why owners rarely see the inside of a workshop.

OwnershipWhy people keep them

The ES 350 wins on the boring metrics that matter over years: cabin noise around 66 dB at highway speed, a V6 that will happily see 200,000 miles on regular servicing, and depreciation curves that flatten unusually early. It is the sedan you buy when you have already been excited by cars and now want the excitement to be optional.
